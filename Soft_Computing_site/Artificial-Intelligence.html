What this area covers

Machine learning and deep learning

Recommender systems, CNN image classification, autoencoders for anomaly detection and generative modelling, and GRU / attention / BERT models for text.

Large language models

Fine-tuning and aligning LLMs, retrieval-augmented generation with FAISS, and LangChain applications for chat and exploratory data analysis.

MLOps and deployment

Data engineering
