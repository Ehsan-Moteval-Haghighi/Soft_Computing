Research area

Computational mechanics

Numerical modelling of materials and structures using finite element methods, from the behaviour of porous media to fracture and multiscale simulation.

Topics

What this area covers

Porous media

Coupled modelling of fluid flow and deformation in porous materials.

Constitutive modelling

Elastic and plastic material models for path-dependent behaviour, and the data needed to calibrate or learn them.

Multiscale simulation

FE² and homogenization approaches that link microstructure to macroscopic response.

Fracture mechanics

Crack initiation and propagation with XFEM and related fracture modelling techniques.
