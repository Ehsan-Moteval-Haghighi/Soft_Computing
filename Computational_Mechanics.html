Computational Mechanics

Solving the differential equations of solids, soils and fluids with finite elements, across scales.

Finite element methods

Nonlinear, coupled finite element solvers built and verified from the governing equations.

Weak form
$$\int_{\Omega} \boldsymbol{\sigma} : \nabla^{s}\delta\mathbf{u}\, d\Omega = \int_{\Gamma_t} \bar{\mathbf{t}}\cdot\delta\mathbf{u}\, d\Gamma$$
Method

Coupled solvers

Monolithic hydro-mechanical and thermo-hydro-mechanical formulations for soils and porous solids.

Method

Nonlinear solution

Newton–Raphson with consistent tangents, adaptive time sub-stepping for stiff, history-dependent problems.

Practice

Verification

Every model checked against analytical solutions or fully resolved direct simulations before it is used.

Porous media

How fluid, heat and deformation interact in soils and rocks, saturated or not.

Biot equations
$$\nabla\!\cdot\!\left(\boldsymbol{\sigma}' - \alpha p\,\mathbf{I}\right) + \rho\mathbf{g} = \mathbf{0}$$
$$\alpha\,\dot{\varepsilon}_v + \tfrac{1}{M}\dot{p} - \nabla\!\cdot\!\left(\tfrac{\mathbf{k}}{\mu}\nabla p\right) = 0$$
Poromechanics

Consolidation

Biot consolidation of layered and double-porosity soils, where clay lenses drain on their own time scale.

Thermo-hydro-mechanics

Unsaturated flow

Moisture and heat flow in unsaturated and fractured media, including climate-driven loading.

Data-driven

Soil–water retention

Retention behaviour of unsaturated soils learned from data with deep networks.

Fracture mechanics

Cracks that open and grow inside partially saturated porous media.

XFEM enrichment
$$\mathbf{u}^h(\mathbf{x}) = \sum_{i} N_i(\mathbf{x})\,\mathbf{u}_i + \sum_{j} N_j(\mathbf{x})\,H(\mathbf{x})\,\mathbf{a}_j$$
Fracture

Cohesive cracks

Cohesive-zone models for crack initiation and propagation coupled with fluid flow in the crack.

Method

XFEM

Extended finite elements that let the crack cut through the mesh without remeshing.

Algorithm

Time sub-stepping

A sub-stepping algorithm that keeps crack propagation stable in variably saturated media.

Multiscale simulation

Linking the microstructure to the structure, and making it fast enough to use.

Hill–Mandel condition
$$\langle \boldsymbol{\sigma} : \delta\boldsymbol{\varepsilon} \rangle_{\Omega_{\mathrm{RVE}}} = \boldsymbol{\Sigma} : \delta\mathbf{E}$$
Method

FE² homogenisation

A microstructure problem (RVE) solved at every integration point of the structure, including transient RVEs for consolidation.

Physics + ML

Neural surrogates

Recurrent networks that replace the RVE inside the macroscale solver, with consistent tangents for Newton convergence.

Physics + ML

Few-shot transfer

A trained surrogate adapted to a new microstructure from a handful of simulations instead of a new dataset.

Constitutive modelling

The stress–strain laws that close the equations, classical or learned from data.

Dissipation inequality
$$\mathcal{D} = \boldsymbol{\sigma} : \dot{\boldsymbol{\varepsilon}} - \dot{\psi} \;\ge\; 0$$
Learned models

Path-dependent plasticity

LSTM and GRU models that learn history-dependent plastic behaviour of heterogeneous, anisotropic materials.

Physics + ML

Thermodynamic consistency

A learned free energy and a dissipation operator that keep the second law satisfied by construction.
