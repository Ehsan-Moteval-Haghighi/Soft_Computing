Research area

Scientific machine learning

Combining physics-based simulation with machine learning to model complex material and physical systems, with a focus on whether the model actually learns the underlying science.

Topics

What this area covers

Physics-informed modelling

Embedding physical laws into neural models: a learned convex free energy and a dissipation operator that keep recurrent surrogates thermodynamically consistent by construction.

Black-box modelling

Data-driven surrogates such as MLP and LSTM models that learn path-dependent stress-strain behaviour from simulation data.

Model-free modelling

Data-driven computational mechanics that works directly with material data, and few-shot transfer of trained surrogates to new microstructures.

Accelerating numerical models

Hybrid AI-FEM and data-driven homogenization to replace expensive sub-scale computations in multiscale simulation.
